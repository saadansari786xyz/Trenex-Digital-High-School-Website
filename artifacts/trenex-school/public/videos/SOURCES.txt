The hero clips are short, silent, local H.264 versions of free Pexels videos. Desktop versions are 1280x720; mobile/low-bandwidth versions are 640x360. Each clip is used for about 5.6 seconds with a 1.25-second crossfade. Replace the matching files in `scenes/` and update the clip array in `src/components/HeroVideoSlideshow.tsx` when changing footage.
